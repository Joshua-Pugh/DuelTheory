# Applied starters — November 6, 2026

Revision: starter-balance-2026-11-06. These are playtest changes, not a claim that the decks are finally balanced. Start a new duel; existing saves are preserved and keep their old deck composition.

## Changes

Standfast: Shieldbearer Noble, Banner Guard and Crown Archer. Spearman and Griffin Knight have not gained it in this first pass. Shieldbearer has three copies; the listed original duplicate increases remain in place. Rune of Clarity stays at one copy and now Exhausts as well as paying 1 Oath to Refine 2.

Bring Down the Mighty (one copy): Tactic, 1 generic + 2 Fury. Additional cost: sacrifice a Brute Unit you control. Destroy target enemy Unit with Defense 4 or greater. Costs are paid before Clash; current Defense and target legality are checked again on resolution. Enemy Legendary Units qualify; Stations do not.

Brutes: two Rites of Ragefire and two Ashen Strike. Blood Cauldron retains its two-sacrifice 4/4 Charge ability and gains a separate Exhaust ability for a 1/1 Frenzied Brute. Fury Offering makes two such tokens, or three if you have no Units at resolution. Basic tokens enter fatigued unless another effect grants Charge. The Keep still replaces up to three destroyed Brutes per turn.

Nobles: two Enforce the Law and two Purge the Unworthy. Court Strategist and the Plan/Refine engine remain. Royal Crusher and Skarn remain the six-Essence legendary finishers. Garrett's original intended effect is unchanged in reserve.

Cost changes: Bloodpit Initiate 1 generic + 1 Fury; Warhowl Instigator 1 generic + 2 Fury; Watchtower Scout 1 generic + 2 Oath. Reserved Korgath and his Call each cost 4 generic + 3 Fury; reserved Garrett 4 generic + 3 Oath; reserved Seraphine 3 generic + 3 Oath. Reserve prices still need playtesting alongside future Command support.

## Token supply and sacrifice

Brutes retain 14 card copies that can create tokens, across 12 designs: Raider ×2, Initiate ×2, Feral Axebreaker, Battlefield Howler, Pit-Sired Warmonger, Ashclaw Skyreaver, Skarn, Pit of the Frenzied, Blood Cauldron, Fury Offering, Bloodforge Hollow and Skullsplitter's Roar. The Keep adds its capped replacement trigger. Six starter designs spend sacrifices: Bring Down the Mighty, Blood Cauldron, Bonespike Hurler, Ashclaw Skyreaver, Pit-Digger's Howl and Maw Below. This preserves recovery and fuel; actual draws and the opportunity cost of losing blockers still need match testing.
