DuelTheory Table Companion Privacy

Ashen Forge Games · Updated October 28, 2026

This notice covers the Table Companion mobile test build and its shared-table service. Some test builds include optional account sign-in. Guest tables do not require an account. Purchases, advertisements and advertising trackers are not included.

Information used by the tool

Your device stores your display name, saved table profiles, preferences and room reconnection credentials. Creating or joining a table sends the name you choose, room settings, named damage sources and game entries to our shared-room service. Other participants in that room can see the shared player names and game state. Use a nickname and do not enter sensitive personal information into table labels.

The room service runs on Cloudflare. Requests include normal network information, including an IP address, which is used to operate and protect the service and limit repeated room-creation attempts. Connections use HTTPS. We do not sell your table information or use it for advertising.

Optional accounts and cloud setups

When account sign-in is enabled and you choose to use it, Supabase processes your email address, authentication records and the game or player setups you explicitly save to your account. Player setups can include a display name and accent color. Resend delivers verification emails. Your device stores a session so you can stay signed in; do not share this session or your verification codes. Cloud setups are private to your account and remain until you delete them or your account. Loading a cloud setup imports a copy onto that device; signing out does not erase local copies.

Delete cloud setups or your account through Your account in Settings & support. Account deletion removes the authentication account and saved cloud setups. Local copies and shared guest rooms are separate. Provider security records and backups follow provider retention schedules. See our account deletion instructions for an alternative email request.

Retention and control

Shared rooms expire after 24 hours without a change, and their stored room data is scheduled for deletion. Clearing the app's storage removes its local profiles and reconnection details; this does not immediately delete a shared room or other players' copies of information. Disconnecting leaves the local room view. Hosting providers may separately process operational network and security records.

For a data question or deletion request, email designteam@ashenforgegames.com. Include the room code if relevant. Never send your seat token or other login credentials. We may need enough information to confirm that a request concerns your data before acting.

Optional device features

Keep-screen-awake is used while the table is visible and enabled. Joining through a QR invite can use your phone's camera application; the table tool does not upload camera images. Profile imports and exports occur only when you choose them.

Support and changes

If payments or additional data features are introduced, this notice will be updated to describe them. Contact Ashen Forge Games at the email above for support.
