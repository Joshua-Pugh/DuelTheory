DuelTheory Table Companion Privacy

Ashen Forge Games · Updated November 4, 2026

This notice covers Table Companion on the website and mobile test build, its shared-table service, and the browser Event Hub pilot. Some test builds include optional account sign-in. Guest tables do not require an account. Purchases, advertisements and advertising trackers are not included.

Information used by the tool

Your device stores your display name, saved table profiles, preferences and room reconnection credentials. Creating or joining a table sends the name you choose, room settings, named damage sources and game entries to our shared-room service. Other participants in that room can see the shared player names and game state. Use a nickname and do not enter sensitive personal information into table labels.

The room service runs on Cloudflare. Requests include normal network information, including an IP address, which is used to operate and protect the service and limit repeated room-creation attempts. Connections use HTTPS. We do not sell your table information or use it for advertising.

Optional accounts and cloud setups

When account sign-in is enabled and you choose to use it, Supabase processes your email address, authentication records and the game or player setups you explicitly save to your account. Player setups can include a display name, accent color and life-panel theme. Resend delivers verification emails. Your device stores a session so you can stay signed in; do not share this session or your verification codes. Cloud setups are private to your account and remain until you delete them or your account. Loading a cloud setup imports a copy onto that device; signing out does not erase local copies.

Delete cloud setups or your account through Your account in Settings & support. Account deletion removes the authentication account and saved cloud setups. Local copies and shared guest rooms are separate. Provider security records and backups follow provider retention schedules. See our account deletion instructions for an alternative email request.

Event Hub store pilot

Event hubs are separate from regular guest rooms. We store the event name, game setup, table identities, player nicknames, named damage sources, totals, announcements and help requests on Cloudflare so the hub and its table QR codes can be reused. People with the event link can see table availability and counts. Joined players can see their own table; the organizer can see all tables and their recent histories. Use nicknames for store demos.

Event data does not have the regular room expiry. Each table keeps up to 80 current history entries. The last three archived games appear in the dashboard; older saved games remain on the server and can be downloaded by the organizer. Round summaries, round timing, and organizer corrections with their explanations are also stored. Ending a round saves its games without removing players. Preparing a new group frees its seats and removes access for the former players. Contact us with the event code for a data or deletion request.

Organizer access is stored as a private credential in the browser that creates the hub. It is not yet linked to a DuelTheory account. Clearing that browser data can lose organizer access; deleting an account does not delete a separate event hub. Public event links and printed QR codes do not include organizer access. Event player colors are shared with their table; uploaded background sharing is currently available only in regular Table Companion rooms.

Retention and control

Regular shared rooms expire after 24 hours without a change, and their stored room data is scheduled for deletion. Clearing the app's storage removes its local profiles and reconnection details; this does not immediately delete a shared room or other players' copies of information. Leaving a table removes your seat and its uploaded background. A host can transfer hosting or close the table for everyone. Shared history can retain earlier entries until the room expires. Hosting providers may separately process operational network and security records.

For a data question or deletion request, email designteam@ashenforgegames.com. Include the room code if relevant. Never send your seat token or other login credentials. We may need enough information to confirm that a request concerns your data before acting.

Table appearance and uploaded images

You can select colors and themes and upload a life-panel background. The app resizes and re-encodes selected images before sending them to the room service. Only members of the open room can retrieve its backgrounds. The active image is removed when its owner leaves, removes it, or the host closes the room; otherwise it expires with the room. You can preview and save an image from Account without joining a room. Saving that preview keeps it on the device. If you enable Use this look when I create or join a table, the saved image and colors are applied to new seats you join; existing seats are not changed just by reconnecting. Otherwise you can apply the saved image manually at the table. A reusable image copy remains on that device until you remove and save it from Account, choose Forget saved image at a table, or clear app storage. Background images are not saved to your Supabase account; cloud player setups save colors and theme preferences.

Optional device features

Keep-screen-awake is used while the table is visible and enabled. Joining through a QR invite can use your phone's camera application; the table tool does not upload camera images. Profile imports and exports occur only when you choose them.

Event demonstration data

The organizer can fill tables with labeled simulated players. These are demonstration records, not additional user accounts. Organizer and player views may store separate reconnection credentials in the same browser. Removing an event table releases its seats and hides it from the directory; archived games remain saved, and the organizer can restore the table with fresh seats. Deleting a whole event similarly closes all tables and releases all seats. It moves to the organizer browser’s deleted-event list and can be restored with fresh seats; this is not permanent erasure of archived data. A tab stores identifiers for event popups already shown so refreshing does not repeatedly show the same notification.

Support and changes

If payments or additional data features are introduced, this notice will be updated to describe them. Contact Ashen Forge Games at the email above for support.
