Delete your DuelTheory account

Ashen Forge Games

In the app

Open Settings & support → Your account → Delete account. Sign in, type DELETE and confirm. This permanently removes your authentication account and saved cloud setups.

Request deletion by email

If you cannot use the app, email designteam@ashenforgegames.com from the email associated with your account and request DuelTheory account deletion. We may ask you to verify ownership before processing the request. Never send passwords, verification codes, API keys or room seat tokens.

What is separate

Local setups and preferences remain on your devices until you remove them or clear app storage. Shared guest rooms expire after 24 hours without changes; deleting an account does not remove other players' copies of shared information. Hosting providers may retain operational security records and backups according to their retention schedules.

Guest users without accounts can contact the same support address about shared-room data.
